      Make a song to sing on (60 seconds)

      • 1. Tap a vibe. Hit Reggaetón · Moody (or another) up top — it sets the key, chords, beat and tempo in one tap. Then make it yours.
      • 2. Own the drums. Swap the Beat, or click the grid — kick's on the floor, dembow snare gives the bounce, shaker adds flavor. Right-click a drum hit to accent it (louder, with a white dot). Click a track's name to mute it.
      • 3. Pick your sound + key. Try Sound → E-Piano or Nylon Pluck for the chords. Sing lower? Drop the Key to A or G — chords always stay right, and your melody transposes with you.
      • 3½. Draw a hook. In Melody, click the grid to draw notes — Stay in key keeps every note in your scale. Drag notes to move them, drag the right edge for length, right-click for loud/soft. Each section has its own melody.
      • 4. Build a song. Make Sections (verse/chorus/bridge — up to 6), arrange them across 32 bars in Song, and let Auto-fill drop a snare build into the last bar before every section change.
      • 5. Record + export. Headphones on → ● Record (count-in, then sing) → Export WAV for voice + backing in one file.
      • 6. Never lose it. Your track auto-saves in this browser, and Copy link puts the whole song in a URL you can text yourself or a friend.

      Make a song in 60 seconds

      1. Choose a sound. Pick a vibe in the Browser — it sets key, chords, beat and tempo in one tap.

      2. Build the beat. In Beat, click the pads. Right-click a drum hit to accent it. Click a track name to mute it.

      3. Draw a melody. In Melody, click to draw. Stay in key keeps every note right. Drag to move, drag the right edge for length, right-click for loud/soft.

      4. Arrange. In Song, click bars to place sections. Auto-fill adds a snare build before each change.

      5. Record. In Vocals, headphones on, press Record and sing after the count-in. Nudge Sync if your voice sits early or late.

      6. Export. Export WAV for audio, or ♪ for MIDI.

      Shortcuts: Space play · R record · M metronome · 1–5 switch view · [ ] previous/next section · Cmd/Ctrl+Z undo · Cmd/Ctrl+S save.
