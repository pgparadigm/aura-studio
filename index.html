Make a song to sing on (60 seconds)

  • 1. Tap a vibe. Hit Reggaetón · Moody (or another) up top — it sets the key, chords, beat and tempo for you. That's a full groove in one tap.
  • 2. Make it yours. Swap the Beat (Dembow, Reggaetón, pop…), nudge Tempo (92 is the reggaetón home base), and play with the drum rows — kick's on the floor, the dembow snare gives the bounce, shaker adds the flavor.
  • 3. Key to your voice. Sing lower? Drop the Key to A or G. The chords always stay right.
  • 4. Headphones on, Record. Hit ● Record — count-in, then sing. Watch the meter so you're not too quiet/loud.
  • 5. Review + export. Play take + music to check it, nudge Sync if you're off, then Export WAV to bounce voice + backing into one file.
