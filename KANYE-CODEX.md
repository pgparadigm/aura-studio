# Kanye Codex — production principles for Aura Studio

Scope note: everything below is grounded in the verified research (corrections marked "wrong" have been applied — e.g. Stronger is E♭ minor, not B♭ minor; Follow God's source is 1974, not 1969; the Runaway "VP-880" vocoder claim is dropped). All step indices are 0-indexed on Aura's 16-step grid. Swing conversion used throughout: MPC-style swing S% ≈ Aura swing = (S−50) × 2 (delay of off-16ths as % of one step). So MPC 54% ≈ Aura 8, MPC 58% ≈ Aura 16, MPC 62% ≈ Aura 24, true 12/8 triplet feel ≈ Aura 33. Every Kanye groove fits inside Aura's 0–60 swing range.

## 1. The five eras in one page (era -> sonic identity -> the ONE transferable principle each)

| Era | Years | Sonic identity | The ONE transferable principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chipmunk soul → stadium** (Dropout / Late Registration / Graduation) | 2004–07 | Soul records pitched up +3 to +5 semitones so the vocal becomes the hook; boom-bap snares hard on beats 2/4, swung hats, live bass under the flip; Brion orchestration; then 80s synths at stadium scale. 80–107 BPM, flat minor keys (E♭m, Fm, F♯m, A♭m, B♭m). | **Make the sample do the hook**: one bright, high-voiced melodic cell (chord stabs in octave 5, soul-style 7ths) is the chorus; verses strip it to fragments. Hook = fullest statement, verse = fewest layers. |
| **808s & Heartbreak** | 2008 | TR-808 as lead instrument; pitched, distorted "heartbreak" kick/bass; Auto-Tune as emotional timbre; static minor harmony; machine-straight grid (swing 0); mix left small and empty on purpose. Two feels: ~87–88 BPM mourn, 120 BPM heartbeat pulse. | **Subtract the backbeat, tune the drum**: delete the snare, let a pitched long-decay 808 carry both rhythm and chord roots, and let silence do the emotional work. |
| **Maximalist** (MBDTF / Watch the Throne) | 2009–11 | Opulent layering — choirs, strings, brass, Moog — stacked over a dry, simple, never-changing funk-break chassis. One austere repeated cell (single E5 piano note) inflated by accretion. 85–95 BPM soul zone; 125–155 anthem zone felt half-time. Black-key minors. | **Grandeur never costs groove**: the groove layer stays dry, loud, unchanging; only the sustained "grandeur" layers get reverb and dynamics. Density = timbre count, never note count. |
| **Industrial-minimal** (Yeezus) | 2013 | Distorted acid leads, four-on-the-floor thud, two-chord negative-space verses, screams as percussion, hard-cut "eruptions" of beauty (gospel choir, dancehall) with zero crossfade. 94–141 BPM, machine-straight. | **Finish by deletion**: for every loop, remove layers until one element carries rhythm and one carries pitch — then make contrast structural (whole-section hard cuts), not textural. |
| **Gospel** (TLOP / JIK / Donda) | 2016–21 | Choir as lead instrument, church organ stabs, harmonic-minor V7 pull, plagal amen moves, drums withheld entirely or released only at the coda; choir processed like a sample. ~72–113 BPM, 12/8 lean. | **Harmony replaces drums**: when the chord loop contains a real dominant (V7 with the raised leading tone), the pull-and-release carries groove tension, so the backbeat becomes optional. |

The single DNA strand across all five: a warm human harmonic core (pitched soul, gospel chords, analog bass) set against a rigid processed machine frame — and each landmark track is built on exactly ONE strong opposition (sacred/profane, dry/wet, one-note/choir, silence/blast).

## 2. Craft laws

1. **Snare on beats 2 and 4 is non-negotiable in soul mode.** Every 2004–07 groove locks layered clap+snare to steps 4 and 12; feel comes from swinging the hats, never the backbeat (MPC swing delays even 16ths; kicks stay on-grid — Soundfly on MPC swing; College Dropout drum programming, Red Bull Music Academy).
2. **Pitch-up lands you in flat minor keys at 83–96 BPM.** Sampling soul at 65–80 BPM and lifting +3 to +5 semitones lands the hip-hop pocket and clusters keys on black-key minors — Jesus Walks 87/E♭m, All Falls Down 91, Gold Digger 92, Flashing Lights 90/F♯m (songbpm/Tunebat, verified).
3. **The drum pattern never gets busier as the arrangement grows.** MBDTF adds choir, strings, brass, Moog in waves while the break stays fixed — Power's breakbeat ticks unchanged under 5,000 man-hours of layering (Wikipedia, Power).
4. **Groove dry, grandeur wet.** Marroquin refused to EQ the 808s "bigger"; Runaway keeps drums dry while the vocoder/string coda swims — reverb belongs to sustained layers only (Rolling Stone, making of 808s; Wikipedia, Runaway).
5. **The tuned 808 IS the harmony.** On 808s & Heartbreak the kick/bass was micro-pitch-tuned so "the 808s [were] changing keys and doing chord changes of the songs" — bass and kick must read as one instrument (Andrew Dawson, DJBooth).
6. **Two to four chords, looped forever; melody supplies all motion.** Heartless cycles B♭m–A♭–Fm–G♭ for its whole length; Ultralight Beam is exactly Cm–E♭–A♭–G7; Runaway loops E–B–C♯m–A (Wikipedia; Hooktheory; Musicnotes).
7. **The V7 is what makes it church.** G7's B-natural, borrowed from C harmonic minor, is the single note creating Ultralight Beam's yearning pull — natural-minor v would make it lo-fi, not gospel (Hooktheory).
8. **Withhold the drums, release them at a structural boundary.** Praise God's drill drums were deleted between listening parties; Jail holds drums 5+ minutes; Use This Gospel pushes them to the coda (Rolling Stone, JIK).
9. **Contrast arrives as a hard cut, never a transition.** On Sight slams into a gospel choir and back with zero crossfade; Blood on the Leaves detonates TNGHT horns at 1:00 with no riser — the edit itself is the effect (Wikipedia, On Sight / Blood on the Leaves).
10. **Silence before the blast.** The Power/Yeezus move: mute everything for one full beat before a hook, then hit step 0 with everything at max velocity; I Am a God weaponizes true digital silence where a fill should be (Yeezus analyses; MBDTF).
11. **Degrade the frame, keep the core warm.** Stronger was run through a cassette deck for grit and mixed ~75 times; Yeezus clips every surviving sound — saturation on drums/master, never on the harmonic core's tuning (Rolling Stone/Billboard, Stronger; Newsweek, Rubin).
12. **Volume of reps beats preciousness.** "Five beats a day for three summers" (~1,300+ beats before anyone heard of him) built the taste; a one-tap preset system is that ethic encoded (Spaceship, 2004; AmbrosiaForHeads 1997 beat tape).

## 4. What Aura already gets right (map existing features to Kanye principles)

| Aura feature | Kanye principle it embodies |
|---|---|
| Bass follows chord root −24 st; '808' voice = sine + pitch-drop + long sustain | Dawson's tuned-808s-carry-the-chord-changes, natively. Aura's 808 bass IS the 808s & Heartbreak "heartbreak" voice minus drive. |
| Kick sidechain-ducks the bass bus | Kick and 808 reading as one instrument (Law 5) — the 808s-era glue is already wired. |
| Swing 0–60% on off-16ths only | Exactly the MPC mechanism: backbeat straight, off-16ths delayed. Whole Kanye swing vocabulary (MPC 54–66%) fits at Aura 8–33. |
| 'soul' chord style auto-adds diatonic 7th | Gospel extension voicing (Law 6/7) — G7 in harmonic minor comes free once the scale exists. |
| boombap preset: kick[0,6,10] snare[4,12] hat[evens] | Snare-on-2-and-4 law, syncopated kick — the College Dropout chassis verbatim. |
| 6 patterns + 32-bar song grid | Beat-switch architecture (Mercy/Blood on the Leaves/Famous) and quiet-loud A/B (Love Lockdown) are sequencable today. |
| Per-track mute + per-hit accents | Strip/stack arrangement and ghost-note dynamics — verse=few lanes, hook=all lanes. |
| Auto-fill snare builds before section changes | Gospel/soul crescendo-re-entry (Never Let Me Down, Selah) built in. |
| Mix bus: glue comp, presence scoop, limiter | Drums-forward saturated glue; "crunchy MPC hit" bus behavior. |
| VIBES one-tap presets | The five-beats-a-day ethic: constrained instant defaults, reps over preciousness. |
| Reverb w/ pre-delay, per-voice sends implied by design | Groove-dry/grandeur-wet split is achievable by policy (cap drum sends, push chord sends). |

Biggest gaps: no harmonic-minor scale (blocks the gospel V7), no tom/taiko voice (blocks Skinhead/Lockdown fills/Monster), no chord-bus sidechain (blocks Graduation/Stronger pump), no drumless-until-coda arrangement macro (blocks Donda), no master drive (blocks Yeezus).

## 5. Ranked build list (top 10, ranked by feel-impact per line of code)

Assume VIBES fields: `{key, mode, prog, beat, bpm, swing, reverb, cs, bs}`; reverb given 0–1 (scale to engine range); steps 0-indexed.

**1. SHIP-NOW — "Lockdown" heartbeat pack (data only, ~6 lines).**
- BEATS `heartbeat`: kick `[0,4,8,12]`, snare `[]`, clap `[]`, hat `[]`, shaker `[2,6,10,14]` at accent-low. The deleted backbeat IS the preset.
- PROGS `lockdown: [0,3,0,3]` (i–iv vamp, C♯m→F♯m over pedal).
- VIBES `heartbreak808: {key:'C#', mode:'minor', prog:'lockdown', beat:'heartbeat', bpm:120, swing:0, reverb:0.12, cs:'piano', bs:'808'}`.
- Highest feel-per-line in the codex: one tap gives users the most imitated beat template of the last 15 years, and Aura's 808 voice + sidechain already do the hard part.

**2. SHIP-NOW — harmonicMinor scale + Ultralight vibe (~10 lines).**
- New scale `harmonicMinor: [0,2,3,5,7,8,11]` (the 11 = raised leading tone is the entire point; 'soul' cs then voices V as a true V7 automatically).
- PROGS `ultralight: [0,2,5,4]` (i–III–VI–V7).
- BEATS `gospelPulse`: kick `[0]`, clap `[8]`, shaker `[]`, hat `[]` — drums almost entirely silence.
- VIBES `ultralight: {key:'C', mode:'harmonicMinor', prog:'ultralight', beat:'gospelPulse', bpm:74, swing:33, reverb:0.55, cs:'soul', bs:'sub'}`. Swing 33 approximates the 12/8 triplet feel on the 16-grid.

**3. SHIP-NOW — "Chipmunk" soul-flip vibe (data only, ~4 lines).**
- PROGS `soulflip: [0,6,5,6]` (i–♭VII–♭VI–♭VII, the F-minor-family flip).
- VIBES `chipmunk: {key:'Eb', mode:'minor', prog:'soulflip', beat:'boombap', bpm:88, swing:16, reverb:0.20, cs:'soul', bs:'sub'}` — 88 BPM is the era median, E♭ minor the era's home key, swing 16 ≈ MPC 58%. Reuses the existing boombap beat verbatim.

**4. Half-time trap/anthem beats + Throne vibe (data only, ~8 lines).**
- BEATS `halftime`: kick `[0,6,10]`, snare `[8]` only, hat all 16 with accents on `[0,4,8,12]`, open hat `[]`. Snare on step 8 alone is the entire Power/Famous/Paris half-time trick.
- PROGS `throne: [0,6,5,4]` (i–♭VII–♭VI–v).
- VIBES `throne: {key:'A#', mode:'minor', prog:'throne', beat:'halftime', bpm:140, swing:8, reverb:0.15, cs:'pluck', bs:'808'}` and `power: {key:'C', mode:'phrygian', prog:'soulflip', beat:'halftime', bpm:77, swing:0, reverb:0.25, cs:'pluck', bs:'sub'}`.

**5. Tom voice (new synth function, ~15 lines).** Sine 180→55 Hz pitch-drop over 80 ms, 5 ms click, 250 ms decay, soft-clip drive 30%. Unlocks:
- BEATS `skinhead`: tom `[0,4,8,12]` full velocity + ghost toms `[7,15]` at accent-low, NO hats, snare `[]`, kick `[]`.
- VIBES `skinhead: {key:'Bb', mode:'minor', prog:'lockdown', beat:'skinhead', bpm:130, swing:33, reverb:0.05, cs:'pluck', bs:'sub'}` (swing 33 = the 12/8 stomp).
- Also add tom fills to `heartbeat` variant: toms on `[10,11,13,15]` every 4th bar (taiko fill).

**6. Chord-bus sidechain ("stadium duck", ~10 lines).** Extend the existing kick→bass ducker to optionally duck the chord bus −30% gain, 60 ms release. VIBES `stadium: {key:'Eb', mode:'minor', prog:'lockdown', beat:'fourfloor', bpm:104, swing:0, reverb:0.35, cs:'pad', bs:'sub'}` with BEATS `fourfloor`: kick `[0,4,8,12]`, clap `[4,12]`, open hat `[2,6,10,14]`. Stronger's key corrected to E♭ minor. This one flag turns pads into Graduation.

**7. Blackout-before-the-blast (engine tweak, ~10 lines).** Optional per-song flag: on the last bar before a song-grid section change, hard-mute all buses on steps 14–15 (true silence, kill reverb tail), then play step 0 of the new section with all accents forced max. Complements the existing auto-fill snare (offer either fill OR blackout). This is Law 10 as one boolean.

**8. Donda mode (arrangement macro, ~20 lines).** A song-grid toggle that mutes kick/snare/clap/hat lanes for the first N bars (default 24 of 32) leaving bass + chords + shaker, then unmutes everything at bar N+1 — the drums-erupt-as-coda structure of Jail/Use This Gospel. Ships with VIBES `donda: {key:'G', mode:'minor', prog:'heartless', beat:'halftime', bpm:80, swing:0, reverb:0.45, cs:'pad', bs:'808'}` and PROGS `heartless: [0,6,4,5]` (i–♭VII–v–♭VI).

**9. 'choir' chord voicing style (moderate, ~30 lines).** Two saws detuned ±8 cents through a bandpass 300 Hz–3 kHz with a formant bump near 800 Hz, attack 180 ms, release 500 ms, default reverb send high. Add a "stab" variant (attack 5 ms, gate short) for Hurricane's choir-cut-like-a-sample. Makes vibes 2, 8 dramatically more convincing; also serves the gospel arc's call-and-response when alternated with muted bars.

**10. "Grit" master knob (Yeezus/cassette, ~25 lines).** One 0–100 macro pre-limiter: waveshaper drive on drum bus (0→60%), soft-clip on chord bus at −3 dB ceiling, gentle high-shelf −3 dB above 8 kHz plus subtle 0.4 Hz pitch-wobble on the reverb return (wow). At low settings it's the Stronger cassette pass; at high it's Yeezus. Keep reverb sends pre-drive so tails come back dirty. VIBES `yeezus: {key:'A', mode:'phrygian', prog:'lockdown', beat:'fourfloor', bpm:102, swing:0, reverb:0.05, cs:'pluck', bs:'808'}` — phrygian's ♭2 is the Send It Up siren interval, and Aura already has the scale.

(If an 11th slot opens: a "harmonic rhythm ÷2" flag letting a PROG's 4 degrees span 2 bars instead of 1 — Kanye loops are mostly one chord per bar, and this halves the harmonic speed of every vibe above for +1 authenticity at ~5 lines.)

## 6. What NOT to copy (Kanye moves that would break Aura's 60-second promise)

1. **Actual sampling / chipmunk pitch-shift of audio.** Aura is sample-free by design; emulate the flip with key bias (flat minors), +1-octave 'soul' voicings, and swing — never bolt on audio pitch-shifting.
2. **Auto-Tune vocal chains.** No pitch detection exists in the engine; a fake retuner would sound broken. Keep the vocal channel HP+comp and honest; the "heartbreak" feeling comes from the 808 voice and minor vamps instead.
3. **Unquantized 12/8 tempo drift.** Ultralight Beam's bar-by-bar manual alignment is unschedulable per-16th; swing 33 is the correct approximation. Do not add a triplet grid or per-step ms jitter — it breaks the schedulable-per-16th contract.
4. **Nine-minute codas and 12-minute songs.** Runaway (9:07) and Last Call don't fit a 32-bar grid or a 60-second first beat. The miniature versions (Donda mode, Mike Dean-style last-4-bars) capture the feel; long-form does not.
5. **14-vocalist / 20-piece-orchestra layer counts.** Aura's charm is 4 chord voices + 2 basses + 6 drums. The maximalist principle is "density = timbre count" — but past ~8 simultaneous synth voices you buy CPU cost and mud, not grandeur. Fake choirs with one 'choir' voicing style, not voice stacking.
6. **Per-song bespoke mixing (75 mixes, 8–10 engineers).** The fixed mastered bus IS the product. One "Grit" macro, one "Sanctuary"-style reverb balance — never per-song EQ surgery exposed to users.
7. **Mid-song key/tempo changes and cross-tempo eruption cuts.** Blood on the Leaves' lament→detonation works partly through feel-shifts Aura can do (snare position, layer swaps), but literal BPM or key changes mid-song fight the global VIBES model and the scheduler. Hard-cut between patterns in the SAME key/tempo only.
8. **"5,000 man-hours" iteration culture.** The product promise is the opposite: five beats a day, each one usable in 60 seconds. Constrain randomness (known-good step pools, the era key bias), never expose open-ended tweaking depth as the default path.
9. **Silence as a 3-minute structural element.** Say You Will's empty half is album art, not app UX; cap negative-space presets (gospelPulse, heartbeat) at patterns that still audibly loop within 4 bars so a first-time user never thinks the app stalled.
