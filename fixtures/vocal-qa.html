Approximate vocal balance — measured

Fourteen mixes, each built here from stems this file wrote, so the exact content of every layer is known before separation and can be measured after it. Each layer sits at its own frequency, so the energy at that frequency in the output is that layer's survival — no estimation, no listening.

What is measured, in dB relative to the same layer in the untouched mix:

  • Lead suppression — how far the centred lead drops in Keep music. More negative is better.
  • Side retention — how much hard-panned backing survives. Nearer 0 is better.
  • Instrumental damage — what the separation cost the music it was supposed to keep. Nearer 0 is better.
  • Recombination error — Full mix must return the recording unchanged, not a filtered copy.
  • Refusal — a mono recording has no centre to remove and must be refused, not silently processed.

No claim of separation is made or tested. This measures an approximation and reports how approximate it is, including the cases where it barely works.
