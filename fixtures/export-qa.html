Export — what actually lands in the singer's file

Every check renders through __auraSuite.renderExport(), which is the same renderExportBuffer() the Export button uses — the same offline graph as playback. That is why an export matches what you hear, and it is the only reason measuring the buffer here says anything about the WAV a singer gets.

Levels are measured, not asserted by eye: RMS overall, plus RMS after a one-pole low-pass at ~120 Hz and a matching high-pass, so "the low end is in the file" is a number rather than an impression.
