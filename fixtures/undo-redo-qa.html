One Apply is one undo — measured, not assumed

Applying a reconstruction touches more than the part it is named after: the low-end Apply also sets the tempo. The guarantee is that one undo puts all of it back, and that the exported audio comes back with it.

This lives in its own suite rather than inside export-qa.html because it is only trustworthy on a freshly booted app. Inside a longer run the import's async tail — the autosave timer, late card renders — lands at a different point relative to each measurement, and the same sequence produced three different outcomes across three runs. A flaky check on a guarantee this important is worse than no check.
