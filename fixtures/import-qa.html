Import & rebuild QA

Runs the real shipped runtime — ../index.html is loaded in a hidden iframe and its window.__auraRebuild analysis surface is called directly, so this measures what ships and not a copy of it. Test audio is generated from qa-audio.js with a seeded PRNG, so a score only moves when the engine moves. Nothing here touches a project, localStorage, or the network.

What each expectation means

exact — the lane is genuinely separable from a mix and must be found on those steps.
family — snare and clap share a lane group in Aura, so either answer is correct.
fallback — the voice is honestly ambiguous (rimshot, shaker, conga). The correct answer is the broad Percussion lane or a Needs review flag. A confident specific lane is a mislabel, which is the failure a singer cannot detect for themselves and is therefore weighted hardest.
subset — the material contains subdivisions finer than Aura's 16-step bar. The integer steps must be found; the engine must not invent hits for the rest.
ignore — not scored for lane; the fixture is scored on timing, tempo, key or structure.
