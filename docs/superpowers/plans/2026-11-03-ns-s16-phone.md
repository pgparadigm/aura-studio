# S16 Phone (≤ 480 px) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** on a phone the screen is the same dashboard as the desktop, one panel at a time: tabs Sounds, Arrange, Mix
and Aura; the arrangement scrolls sideways under a sticky track column; the transport is fixed at the bottom.

**Architecture:** at ≤ 480 px the body carries `phonedash`. The dashboard's four regions are the four panels (Sounds is
`#browser`, Arrange is `#studioArr`, Mix is the dock's tab row and editor, Aura is the right panel `#inspect`), one shown
at a time by `data-ptab`. The classic rooms and their bottom bar are hidden. ▶ Play, ● Record and Export move (the same
elements, so every handler and id is unchanged) into a transport bar fixed at the bottom, under the four tabs. The More
menu stays in the header. 481 to 767 px keeps the classic phone shell, as today (the roadmap scopes S16 to ≤ 480).
`DASH_PHONE` and `classicGuided()` now mean that classic range only; at ≤ 480 Guided is `gmode`, as on a desktop.

**Spec:** roadmap row S16 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-aura-north-star.md`); Philip, 2026-11-03: "Finish
everything before I review it."
