# S8 Sounds panel Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the Sounds column of `docs/mockups/studio-dashboard-target.png`: "Sounds" with a clear-filters icon and a
collapse chevron; search with its icon; Grounded, Radiant and Dreamlike on one row in their own colours; three featured
preset cards (a thumbnail drawn on the device, name, tags, ▶, a mini waveform); the Project intention card; Import audio
and Record sound; Local project at the foot. At 1120-1399 px (1280 among them) the column is an icon rail and the panel
opens over the arrangement.

**Architecture:** the column is `#browser` (`#soundsChrome` above the older "ways in" `.rbody`); the cards are built in
the dashboard section of `rc/app.js` (`#featPresets`). Thumbnails are canvas, drawn from the card's colour; each
waveform is the card's own preview phrase rendered once in an OfflineAudioContext with the app's `playChord` /
`playBass`. ▶ plays that same phrase live, through the master, without touching the project. The rail reuses the
drawer the column already becomes below 1120 px.

**Spec:** roadmap row S8 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-aura-north-star.md`); Philip, 2026-11-03: "Finish everything
before I review it. Should look like attached."

## Checks (dashboard-n, `n8*`), in the order they are written

1. `n8PreviewPlays`: ▶ on a card sounds (the Master meter moves) and changes nothing: the saved state, the undo depth
   and the chord and bass sounds are as before, and the song does not start. (Today ▶ applies the preset and plays the
   whole song.)
2. `n8CardApplies`: a press on the card itself applies its sound, and Undo takes it back.
3. `n8Cards`: three cards, each with a drawn thumbnail (not blank, unlike the others), name, tags, its own ▶ (not inside
   another button) and a waveform drawn from its sound (not flat, unlike the others); nothing cut.
4. `n8Chips`: the three feelings on one row at 1440, 1512 and 1920, whole, each its own colour; each filters the cards,
   a second press shows all.
5. `n8Search`: search filters the cards and the vibe tiles; the clear-filters icon shows everything again and is
   disabled when nothing is filtered.
6. `n8Intention`: the card shows the intention and "Keep: vocal + main melody" (from the Preserve locks); the pencil
   edits it; Enter keeps it in the project.
7. `n8IconRail@1280` (and 1200, 1399): the column is a rail at most 64 px wide; its Sounds icon opens the panel over the
   arrangement with search and cards on screen; Escape closes it; Import and Record do what the panel's buttons do.
8. `n8Collapse@1440`: the chevron folds the column to the rail and back, and is remembered.
9. `n8Foot`: Local project sits at the foot of the column.
